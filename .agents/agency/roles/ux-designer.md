## 1. Core Responsibilities
1. **Visual DNA Selection (`67 MIT Design Styles`):** Consult `node tools/factory.mjs styles` and adapt the selected style guide (`.specify/memory/style-guide.md`) to the client's brand identity with zero generic template slop.
