# Claude Council — Multi-Perspective Deliberation Protocol

> **Zero-Copy Vault Source:** `C:\Users\tomas\Desktop\streamerbot\references\repos\skills_repos\claude-council`

## Deliberation Protocol (`runCouncilDeliberation` in `agent_squad.mjs`)
1. **Parallel Independent Perspectives:** Consult at least 3 distinct architectural lenses (e.g., *Performance/Edge*, *Zero-Trust Security*, *UX/Conversion*) before committing to a complex design or architecture decision.
2. **Synthesis of Agreement vs. Divergence:** Explicitly separate what all perspectives agree on from where they diverge.
3. **Surface Hidden Premises:** When all perspectives agree, explicitly state the underlying assumption that agreement rests on (and what condition would flip the decision).
